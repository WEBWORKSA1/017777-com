Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and your choices.

Last updated 2026-10-22

Who we are

017777.com (“we”) operates this website. Contact us through the contact page.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (contact, strategy call, newsletter, pledge, contest entry, job application) we receive the details you enter. Form submissions are relayed to our inbox by a third-party form-forwarding service (FormSubmit). We use this information only to respond to you, run contests you enter, process pledges and send newsletters you request. We do not sell personal information.

Tools

Our number tools run in your browser. The numbers, names and dates you type into them are not sent to our servers.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more at how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

We also store small preferences in your browser (theme, cookie choice). Embedded YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play; once played, YouTube's own policies apply.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, or CCPA/CPRA), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and object to certain processing. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising except through the advertising cookies described above, which you can control.

Retention

We keep form messages for up to 24 months, job applications for 12 months and contest entries for 12 months after a contest closes, unless the law requires longer.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and contests are limited to adults.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
