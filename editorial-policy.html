Editorial policy

How we research, write and correct.

Last updated 2026-10-22

Sourcing

Factual claims about research, history, telephony or markets link to a primary or reputable secondary source. We summarise in our own words.

Maths is verified

Every number property on the site is generated by the same open code that powers our decoder, and spot-checked by hand.

Belief, clearly labelled

Numerology and angel-number sections are marked as tradition and paired with the relevant psychology. We never claim numbers predict outcomes.

Independence

Advertisers and sponsors do not see or approve editorial content before publication. Sponsored items are labelled.

Corrections

Found an error? Tell us. We correct factual errors promptly and update the “last reviewed” date.

AI assistance

We may use AI tools to help draft or check content. Every page is reviewed by a person, and facts are checked against sources before publication.
