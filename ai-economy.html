Numbers in AI and the economy

From the zeros and ones under every AI model to the compounding that decides every economy.

Binary: why everything is 0 and 1

Digital hardware is built from switches that are either off or on, so every value is written in base 2. The number 17,777 is 100010101110001 in binary — 15 bits. A byte is 8 bits and can hold 256 values (0–255). Hexadecimal groups bits in fours, which is why 17,777 is the compact 0x4571. Convert any value.

How AI models are made of numbers

  • Tokens. Language models split text into tokens — whole words, word pieces or punctuation — and give each one an integer ID.
  • Embeddings. Each token ID maps to a long list of decimal numbers that captures meaning by position in a high-dimensional space.
  • Parameters. A model's “knowledge” is a very large set of numeric weights adjusted during training. Bigger models have more weights, but data quality and training method matter as much as size.
  • Precision. Weights are stored as 32-, 16-, 8- or even 4-bit numbers. Fewer bits mean smaller, cheaper, faster models with a small accuracy trade-off — a pure numbers decision with large cost consequences.
  • Randomness. “Temperature” controls how strongly a model favours its most likely next token. Low temperature is predictable; high temperature is varied.

The Rule of 72 and compounding

Divide 72 by a growth rate to estimate how many years it takes to double. At 7% that is about 10.3 years (exact: 10.24). The rule works for anything that compounds: investments, prices under inflation, users, revenue, even model training compute.

Numbers that move markets

Economies run on a handful of headline figures: inflation rates, policy interest rates, unemployment, GDP growth and exchange rates. Two numeric habits help when reading them. First, convert rates into doubling or halving times, which are easier to feel. Second, watch round-number thresholds: markets and media react to levels like 2% inflation targets or index milestones because round numbers anchor attention, even when nothing fundamental changed at that exact point.
